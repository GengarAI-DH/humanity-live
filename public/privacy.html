Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Humanity, Live (humanitylivewatch.com) is a live simulation of Earth's history. This page explains what information the site keeps, why, and what you can do about it. We keep as little as we can, and we never sell your data.

What we keep

  • Chat and the ideas board: the name you choose, your messages, your ideas and your votes. The last 500 chat messages are kept; older ones are deleted automatically. Messages that several people report are removed.
  • Spam protection: a scrambled code made from your internet (IP) address. It is used only to slow down spam and enforce bans. We don't store the address itself.
  • Trading cards: a random device code stored in your browser, the cards you've collected with their serial numbers, your collector name, and your trades. If you sign in with Google, we keep Google's account ID for you and your first name, so your cards follow you across devices. We do not receive your password, your email or your contacts.
  • Alerts: if you turn on alerts, your browser's push address and the species or events you follow.
  • On your device only: settings, favorites, badges and your chat name are stored in your browser and never sent to us except as described above.

Visit statistics and error reports

We use PostHog to count anonymous page views and which features get used, and Sentry to receive error reports when something breaks, so we can keep the site fast and working. We don't use session recordings, and these tools don't receive your chat messages or cards.

Advertising

The site may show ads from Google AdSense. If it does, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads and measure them. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent first. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Backups

To protect the shared world and everyone's cards, the server makes a nightly backup that is kept for 30 days and then deleted.

Children

Chat, the ideas board and trading are for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has posted, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's site data to remove your device code and settings.
  • Email us to delete your chat messages, ideas, cards or account information.
  • Turn alerts off at any time in the app.
