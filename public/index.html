The clock

Catching up…

Earth’s history, live.

It begins 71 million years ago, in the last age of the dinosaurs, and runs on the real clock with no way to speed it up: about 2 years and 3 months to reach 2100. For the dinosaurs one second is a year; the clock slows as humans arrive, so history plays out at a pace you can watch (from 1800, one year takes about 10 hours). The asteroid hits on January 26. Then mammals take over, the continents drift into place, early humans appear, and on the last day 100 Homo sapiens set out from East Africa and every person is simulated through today into a projected 2100.

Big events are announced 30 days ahead with a countdown and a glowing hotspot so you can be there to watch.
