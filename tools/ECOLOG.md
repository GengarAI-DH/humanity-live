# Ecology log (newest first)

How to add interactions: see the "about" field in public/interactions.json for kinds. One real source per rule, keys must exist in the page (SP in tools/tpl.html, wildAt0/EXTRA, ANI, public/species.json). Note: 'seal' (sea) is defined but never spawned, so rules targeting it do nothing; use 'sealland'.

## Nov 28, 2026 (sixth run)
- Added 366 rules (1748 -> 2114). New kind: grooms (land: an animal walks up to a group-mate, settles beside it and grooms it; the groomed one stands still and its card says "being groomed by a group-mate"). Used for baboons (Henzi & Barrett 1999), chimpanzees (Goodall 1986), gorillas, macaques (Dunbar 1991), lemurs, orangutans, horses and Przewalski's horses (Feh & de Mazieres 1993), zebra (Penzhorn 1984), impala/antelope (Hart & Hart 1992), cattle (Sato et al. 1991), meerkats (Kutsukake & Clutton-Brock 2006), lions (Schaller 1972), wolves (Mech 1970), giraffe, elephants (Moss 1988), hyenas (Kruuk 1972).
- Every Cenozoic species that had no rule now has one or more: Australia (Thylacoleo and Megalania on short-faced kangaroos, Horton & Wright 1981 cut marks; Wroe 2002; Riversleigh Wakaleo, Nanowana, Baru; giant megapodes; Paludirex), South America (Santa Cruz terror birds and sparassodonts on Protypotherium and Hapalops, Vizcaino et al. 2012; Andalgalornis, Degrange et al. 2010; Protocyon packs, Prevosti et al. 2005; carnivorous armadillos Macroeuphractus and Peltephilus as scavengers; Panochthus tail fights, Alexander et al. 1999; La Venta Purussaurus), Africa (Dinofelis on Cercopithecoides, Brain 1981; Chasmaporthetes, Megantereon, Homotherium on Plio-Pleistocene pigs, horses and kob; Pelorovis fights), proboscidean musth fights (Poole 1987, close-relative inference), Zhoukoudian Pachycrocuta and Sinomegaceros (Boaz et al. 2004), Basilosaurus and Saghacetus (Voss et al. 2019), sea cows in herds, megalodon and Homiphoca.
- Maastrichtian: one small-theropod predator per uncovered small mammal, lizard and snake by region and age (Hone et al. 2022; O'Connor et al. 2019; noasaurids, Sampson et al. 2001), crocodylians on frogs, salamanders and freshwater turtles (Noto et al. 2012; Carpenter & Lindsey 1980 for Brachychampsa), Hell Creek gars, bowfins and Melvius (Bryant 1989), sawfish (Wueringer et al. 2012), angel sharks (Fouts & Nelson 1999), lamniform sharks on bony fish (Cappetta 2012), Protosphyraena and Xiphactinus (Everhart 2005), Moroccan mosasaurs and Zarafasaura on lizardfish and Dercetis (Bardet et al. 2010; Vincent et al. 2011), shoaling herrings and smelts (Pitcher & Parrish 1993), Bonnerichthys filter feeding (Friedman et al. 2010).
- Checked by script: every key exists in the page, every hunter is a predator in the same habitat, actor and target overlap in time and region, no duplicates of actor+target+kind.
- Gaps: about 120 Maastrichtian sea fish still without a rule (mostly rays and small sharks that eat shellfish the engine does not draw); sea turtles are land-habitat so mosasaurs cannot hunt them; Maastrichtian birds are air-habitat.
