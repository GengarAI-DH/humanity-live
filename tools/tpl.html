The clock

Catching up…

One second = one year.

It begins 71 million years ago, in the last age of the dinosaurs, and runs on the real clock with no way to speed it up: about 2 years and 3 months to reach 2100. In about two months the asteroid hits. Then mammals take over, the continents drift into place, early humans appear, and on the last day 100 Homo sapiens set out from East Africa and every person is simulated through today into a projected 2100.

Big events are announced 30 days ahead with a countdown and a glowing hotspot so you can be there to watch.

It is one shared world. Close it and it keeps going; open it on any device and you will see the same people.
