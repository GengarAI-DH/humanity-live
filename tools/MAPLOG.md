# Map log (cartographer job, newest first)

## 2026-11-27 (later): lakes on their real dates (upgrade 3, first part)
- What: lakes now change with the year. 48 reservoirs in the Natural Earth lakes (Lake Volta, Nasser, Kariba, Mead, Powell, Itaipu, Rybinsk, Kuybyshev/Samara, Bratsk, Smallwood, the La Grande and Missouri reservoirs, Gatun, Salton Sea...) are hidden before their dam closed and fade in over their real filling years (table RESV in tpl.html: [first fill, full, drained?]); Kakhovka drains in June 2023. Four historic outlines are drawn at full size until their decline and fade to today's lakes: Aral Sea (full until 1960, today's remnants by 2000), Lake Chad (1963 to 1987), Lake Texcoco around Tenochtitlan (1600 to 1910, the Huehuetoca drain to the Gran Canal), Lop Nur (1950 to 1972). Lakes whose middle lies under an ice-age ice sheet (the ICE ellipses at the current ice factor) are not drawn, so the Great Lakes, Winnipeg, Great Slave/Bear and the Scandinavian lakes vanish under the ice at the Last Glacial Maximum.
- applyClimate now also repaints when a dated lake changes state (lakeSig: reservoir fill in quarters, historic lakes in eighths; 0 outside 1590-2030 so no extra repaints elsewhere).
- Source: Natural Earth 1:10m Historic lakes (ne_10m_lakes_historic, public domain, naturalearthdata.com; fetched from the nvkelso/natural-earth-vector GitHub mirror), simplified to 0.04 degrees and stored in public/water.json key "h" ([name, full until, gone by, ring]) by tools/lakes_hist.py. Dam and filling dates from the dams' own histories (well known, listed in RESV).
- Numbers: water.json 352 KB -> 360 KB (+7.8 KB, loaded lazily as before); index.html +2.6 KB. No per-frame work (texture painting only). Smoke case 1965 checks dates, the ice test, the 1950 vs 2025 Aral Sea and Lake Volta pixels.
- Next: Natural Earth 10m lakes and rivers (river width by discharge from scalerank/strokeweight), Three Gorges (2003-2006) and other big river reservoirs that 50m data lacks, the Zuiderzee before the 1932 Afsluitdijk and its polders (1930/1942/1957/1968), Mega-Chad and green-Sahara lakes 9000-3500 BC, ice-age proglacial lakes (Agassiz, Missoula, Bonneville) on their dates.

## 2026-11-27: sharper mountains up close (upgrade 1, first part)
- What: 4 arc-minute elevation tiles for six mountain regions, fetched only when the camera is below altitude 14 over one and the year is after 3 million years ago. A 120x120-vertex ground patch (10x10 globe cells, about 7 degrees, 3.5 arc-minutes per vertex) is laid over the 0.5-degree globe around the camera: its edges sit exactly on globe grid lines and use the globe's own vertex heights, so there is no seam; the globe vertices inside it are sunk 1.2 units out of sight. Extra height = (fine - coarse ETOPO) x KMU, faded out at the patch edge, tile edge, coasts (only on land) and around towns (flattened to the town's height within ~0.08-0.24 degrees so buildings stay on the ground). hAt() includes the extra height inside the patch so sites, trees and animals sit on it; hAt0() is the old coarse height (used to build the globe). Patch moves when the camera drifts 2+ globe cells; dropped (globe restored) above altitude 14, in deep time or outside tiles.
- Tiles (public/topo/<name>.bin, raw uint8 rows south to north, 15 samples per degree, value x step metres; table HRT in tpl.html):
  himalaya 26-40N 68-98E step 35 m (95 KB), alps 43-49N 4-17E step 20 (18 KB), andes_n 18S-8N 82-62W step 30 (118 KB), andes_s 45-18S 76-62W step 30 (86 KB), eafrica 12S-15N 28-44E step 25 (98 KB), rockies 32-50N 118-104W step 20 incl. Grand Canyon (57 KB). Total 468 KB, none loaded on first page load.
- Source: NOAA NCEI ETOPO1 Ice Surface 1 arc-minute global relief (Amante & Eakins 2009, NOAA Tech. Memo NESDIS NGDC-24; US government, public domain), every 4th sample, via the NCEI THREDDS OPeNDAP server (www.ngdc.noaa.gov/thredds/dodsC/global/ETOPO1_Ice_g_gmt4.nc.ascii?z[lat0:4:lat1][lon0:4:lon1], index = (deg+90)*60 / (deg+180)*60) through the import queue.
- How to fetch more: queue the OPeNDAP URL, then in the Cloudflare execute tool parse the ascii, quantize to uint8 (step = max/255), delta-encode rows, gzip, base64 and return with truncate:false so the result is saved to a file instead of the conversation; decode with numpy (cumsum mod 256 along rows).
- Numbers: first load unchanged (tiles are lazy). Patch adds ~29k triangles and 1 draw call only while active; built once per move (not per frame). JS ms/frame at Everest alt 3: noise-level change (see commit). Smoke case 2020 checks the tile, patch, edge blend, climate rebuild, restore and deep-time off.
- Next: ETOPO 2022 15-arc-second data (www.ngdc.noaa.gov/thredds/catalog/global/ETOPO2022/) for 2 arc-minute tiles near the most visited peaks; more regions (Caucasus, Pamir/Tian Shan, New Zealand Alps, Japan, Ethiopian highlands, Appalachians, Scandinavia); fine seafloor near trenches; tint the patch by slope (bare rock on cliffs, snow on high ridges) since the 2048x1024 colour texture is much coarser than the new shape.
